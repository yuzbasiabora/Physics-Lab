      Physics Lab: a free, open-source classroom tool (MIT license)Press Space to run or pause

      How to use Physics Lab

      1. Pick a simulation from the tabs at the top.
      2. Type your prediction in “Make a prediction” and press Check it after you run the experiment.
      3. Change the controls (you can type exact values in the number boxes) and watch the readout and graphs.
      4. Press “Record this setup” to build a table, then copy it as CSV for your lab report.
      5. Work through the “Try this” questions.

      For teachers

      • Set up an experiment and press “Copy link”. Students who open the link get exactly that setup.
      • “Print worksheet” makes a handout with the equations, the questions and your recorded data.
      • The answer key is under “Try this” on each simulation.
      • No accounts and no tracking. Everything runs in the browser.
